A newspaper front page that sums up the world's news of Thursday 19
November 2026 in one image: 47 stories across 7 regions — 13 cards
(3 heroes + 10 grid stories) and 34 briefs. Model B — Light
Broadsheet: cream broadsheet paper, giant black serif masthead,
three heroes with big literal scene illustrations, a region bar and
a six-number "day in numbers" strip, a small world map with numbered
pins, and a date-check strip listing the previous day's stories that
were deliberately left out. Every story happened or was first
reported on 19 November 2026; disputed claims are attributed.
Submitted to
[news-image-20261119](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261119) on
make.fun.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

`main.py` is standard library only and copies the pre-rendered
`entry.jpg` to `/output/entry.png` (1024x1024, under 1 MiB).
